Frequently Asked Questions

Is Leago Academy free? ▼
Yes, we offer a free plan with 5 AI Tutor questions per day. For unlimited access, you can upgrade to Premium for R49.99 per month.
How does the AI Tutor work? ▼
The AI Tutor uses a large language model (DeepSeek) to provide personalised, step‑by‑step explanations. It adapts to your grade, curriculum and subject, and remembers your conversation history for continuity.
Which curricula does Leago Academy support? ▼
We currently support CAPS (South Africa), IEB, CBC (Kenya), WAEC (West Africa), and ZIMSEC (Zimbabwe). We are continuously adding more curricula and subjects.
How does Premium work? ▼
Premium costs R49.99 per month and gives you unlimited AI Tutor questions, full access to all subjects and topics, and an ad‑free experience. You can manage your subscription from your dashboard.
Can I cancel my subscription? ▼
Yes, you can cancel your Premium subscription at any time from your dashboard. Cancellation will take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. No refunds are provided for partial months.
How is my data handled? ▼
We take your privacy seriously. Your personal information, conversation history, and usage data are stored securely and used only to provide and improve the service. We do not sell your data to third parties. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.
Can I use Leago Academy on my phone? ▼
Yes! Leago Academy is fully responsive and works well on smartphones, tablets and desktops. The interface adapts to your screen size for a comfortable learning experience.
How do I contact support? ▼
You can reach us at support@leagoacademy.co.za. We aim to respond within 48 hours during weekdays.
